Intra-University Sporting Glory

About Collympics

Sri Sri University proudly presents Collympics, our much-awaited intra-university sports fest. Over the years, Collympics has become more than just a tournament – it is a tradition that brings together students from every department to showcase their passion, strength, and spirit of teamwork.

Collympics is not just about competition—it’s about celebrating energy, unity, and excellence on the field. It provides a platform for budding athletes to test their limits, nurture sportsmanship, and carry forward the spirit of fair play.

Rules & Code of Conduct

Official General Guidelines

Important rules and code of conduct for all participating student athletes.

  1. Eligibility: Open to all students, faculty, and staff of Sri Sri University.
  2. Registration Deadline: Mandatory online registration before closing date (Dates To Be Announced).
  3. ID Verification: All players must carry their valid University ID card at all times.
  4. Team Approval: Team registration must be completed by the Captain of the team.
  5. Reporting Time: Report at venue at least 20 minutes prior to scheduled match time. Walkovers will be awarded for late reporting.
  6. Dress Code & Equipment: Proper sports attire mandatory. Players must bring their own kits.
  7. Game Rules: Matches strictly follow official federation rules (FIBA, FIFA, PKL, KKFI, etc.). Referee decisions are final.
  8. Discipline: Misconduct, fight, or alcohol/drugs will result in immediate disqualification.
  9. Minimum Participation: Team events require at least 5 registered teams (different departments); individual athletics require at least 12 participants.
