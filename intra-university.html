About Collympics 11.0

Sri Sri University proudly presents Collympics 2026, the landmark 11th edition of our much-awaited intra-university sports fest. Over the years, Collympics has become more than just a tournament—it is a cherished tradition that brings together students from every department to showcase their passion, strength, and spirit of teamwork.

This landmark 11th edition is a celebration of a decade of sporting glory at SSU, where champions are made, friendships are forged, and values of discipline, resilience, and unity shine bright. Collympics is about celebrating energy, unity, and excellence on the field, providing a platform for budding athletes to test their limits and nurture sportsmanship.

Key Technical Regulations

  • 3 Attempts Each: Athletes get 3 attempts in Squat, Bench Press & Deadlift.
  • Best Lift Scored: The highest successful lift in each discipline is recorded.
  • Combined Total Ranking: Overall winners decided by the combined total weight of all three lifts.
  • Mandatory Weigh-In: Participants must report for official weigh-in prior to the event.
  • Safety & Referee Decision: Safety instructions strictly enforced; technical committee's verdict is final.

Rules & Code of Conduct

Official General Guidelines

Mandatory guidelines and regulations issued by the Sri Sri University Sports Management Committee.

  1. Eligibility: The tournament is open to all students, faculty, and staff of Sri Sri University.
  2. Registration Deadline: Every participant must fill out the official Collympics 11.0 registration form and pay the registration fee, whether for team or individual events. Registration is strictly mandatory before the closing date: 22nd September 2026. Without valid registration, participation will not be permitted.
  3. ID Verification: All players must carry their valid University ID card at all times during the event for spot verification.
  4. Team Approval: Team registration will be done exclusively by the Captain of the respective team.
  5. Reporting Time & Walkover: All teams and individual participants are required to report at the designated venue at least 20 minutes prior to the scheduled match time with their complete squad. Failure to do so may result in a walkover being awarded to the opponent by the committee.
  6. Dress Code & Equipment: Players must wear proper sports attire for their respective games. All players must bring their own playing kits and standard equipment.
  7. Game Rules: Matches will strictly follow the official rules of the respective sports federations (e.g., FIBA, FIFA, PKL, KKFI, BWF, ITTF, etc.). The decision of referees and appointed officials will be final and binding.
  8. Fixtures: Fixtures for all events will be prepared by the Committee through a transparent lot system. Matches will be conducted strictly as per the fixture schedule. The Sports Committee reserves all rights to alter match dates and timings in case of unfavorable weather or circumstances.
  9. Discipline & Code of Conduct: Any form of indiscipline, misconduct, malpractice, or physical altercation will result in immediate disqualification of the entire team. Consumption of alcohol, smoking, or drugs is strictly prohibited on campus. Any damage caused to university property will lead to penalties and disciplinary action.
  10. Safety & Responsibility: Players must take care of their personal belongings. The university will not be responsible for any lost or misplaced items.
  11. Minimum Participation: Team events require at least 5 registered teams (from different departments) to be conducted. Individual athletics require at least 12 participants.
  12. Committee Authority: The Sports Management Committee reserves all rights to make necessary changes in the schedule, fixtures, and guidelines when deemed necessary.
